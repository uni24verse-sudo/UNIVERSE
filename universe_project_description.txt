PROJECT NAME: UniVerse
TAGLINE: Your Campus, Digitized.

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
UniVerse is a comprehensive, multi-tenant digital ordering and management platform specifically designed for university campus environments. It bridges the gap between students and local campus stalls by digitizing the entire food and services ordering experience, effectively eliminating long queues and introducing modern convenience to daily campus life. 

With a premium, dark-mode-first aesthetic, UniVerse provides a seamless, dynamic, and responsive user experience for both customers and vendors.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
University campuses and large organizational complexes house multiple standalone stalls, canteens, and micro-markets that cater to thousands of students and staff daily. However, the ordering process is historically plagued by several inefficiencies:
- Severe Time Wastage: Students spend a significant portion of their short breaks waiting in long, unmanaged queues just to place an order or pay.
- Manual Error & Ambiguity: Vendors handle orders verbally amidst loud crowds, leading to wrong orders, missed variations, and poor customer satisfaction.
- Inefficient Payment Collection: Handling fragmented UPI transfers, verifying screenshots, and managing loose cash slows down service and complicates end-of-day accounting for stall owners.
- Lack of Visibility: Students often walk to a distant campus block only to find that their favorite stall is closed or out of stock.

UniVerse solves these issues by aggregating campus stalls into a single unified platform. It empowers students to order and pay digitally from anywhere on campus, while giving vendors the technical infrastructure to manage digital menus, accept organized online orders, and seamlessly track their revenue.

3. CORE MODULES & FEATURES

[ For Customers / Students ]
- Discover Local Stalls: Browse localized "Markets" (such as LIT Market, Mall Market, BH1 Market, etc.) to discover which campus stalls are currently open or closed.
- Digital Menus & Ordering: Browse categorized menus, add items (including combo meals and size variants) to a cart, and place orders directly from their phones.
- Seamless Payments: Integrated with Razorpay and UPI. Orders are strictly verified on the backend to ensure data integrity before being sent to the vendor.
- Order Tracking: Real-time status updates (Pending -> Confirmed -> Completed) so students know exactly when to pick up their items.

[ For Vendors / Store Owners ]
- Real-Time Store Management: A dedicated Vendor Dashboard allows stall owners to toggle their store's "Open/Closed" status with a single click.
- AI Magic Scan (Menu Digitzation): Vendors can upload a picture of their physical paper menu, and the system uses AI to instantly scan, price, and import the items directly into their digital storefront.
- Order Notifications: Critical order alerts are pushed instantly to the vendor via OneSignal (push notifications) and Telegram Bots, ensuring no order is ever missed.
- Financial Tracking: Vendors can track their daily/monthly revenue directly within the portal.

[ For Platform Operators / Super Admins ]
- Central Command Panel: A powerful, high-level dashboard to monitor all platform traffic, vendors, and transactions.
- Vendor Verification & Control: Super Admins can ban bad actors, manually override store statuses, and change the priority/ranking of how stores appear on the homepage.
- Automated Billing & Commissions: The platform supports automated billing logic, giving new stores a 30-day free trial before transitioning them to a percentage-based commission model on their transactions.

3. TECHNICAL STACK
- Frontend: Built with React.js using Vite. Styled with vanilla CSS, focusing on modern glassmorphism, fluid micro-animations, and striking gradient accents to ensure a visually impressive "WOW" factor.
- Backend: Node.js with Express framework. Uses WebSockets (Socket.io) for real-time order handshakes.
- Database: MongoDB (Atlas) for flexible, document-based storage of complex schemas (Stores, Products, Orders, Vendor profiles).
- External Integrations:
  - Payments: Razorpay Gateway
  - Notifications: OneSignal (Web Push) & Telegram API
  - Storage: Cloudinary (for storing store images and scanned menus)
