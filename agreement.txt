1. PURPOSE & SCOPE 
UniVerse runs a digital platform that makes it easy for vendors to handle and carry out customer 
orders. Vendors can do the following through this Platform: 
• Get and handle customer orders through QR codes or digital ordering systems  
• Accept digital payments through payment gateways that are built in  
• Keep track of sales, revenue, and order data  
• Use digital tools to make your business run more smoothly  
The Vendor agrees to only use UniVerse to process platform-based orders as part of this Agreement. 

2. FREE TRIAL PERIOD 
• The Vendor shall receive a 30-day free trial period starting from the onboarding date.  
• During the free trial period:  
a) No platform commission shall be charged.  
b) Payment gateway fees (approx. 2%) shall be borne solely by the Vendor.  
• After the trial period, the standard commission structure shall apply automatically.  
• After the free trial, UniVerse will apply a Platform Commission of 5% on the Vendor's total monthly sales made through the Platform.  
• This 5% breakdown is as follows:  
a) 3% Platform Usage Fee (plus applicable 18% GST).  
b) 2% Payment Gateway Processing Fee.  
• There won't be any "hidden" service fees outside this structure.  
• All commissions and taxes will be automatically deducted from monthly payouts.  

4. PAYMENT COLLECTION & SETTLEMENT TERMS 
• All customer payments shall be processed through UniVerse’s integrated payment partners (e.g., 
Razorpay).  
• Payments will first be collected by UniVerse and then transferred to the Vendor after deductions.  
• UniVerse will compute Vendor earnings after:  
a) Deducting monthly commissions  
b) Adjusting refunds, cancellations, or penalties as applicable  
• Settlement Cycle:  
a) Payouts shall be processed once every month.  
b) Settlements will be released within 7 working days after the end of each month.  
• UniVerse shall not be liable for delays caused by banking or payment gateway partners.
