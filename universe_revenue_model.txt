UNIVERSE: REVENUE & MONETIZATION MODEL
=====================================

This document outlines the financial architecture of the UniVerse platform, focusing on how the system generates value and manages cash flows between customers, vendors, and the platform operator.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE CORE REVENUE STREAM: TRANSACTION COMMISSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UniVerse operates on a "Success-Based" monetization model. The platform only earns when the vendors earn. This ensures high vendor adoption and alignment of interests.

- [Standard Commission]: 5% per successful transaction.
- [Model Breakdown]:
    - 2.0% : Payment Gateway Fee (Razorpay/Transaction costs).
    - 3.0% : Platform Service Fee (UniVerse Pure Profit).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE GROWTH ENGINE: 30-DAY FREE TRIAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To lower the barrier to entry for campus stall owners, UniVerse implements a tiered onboarding model:

- [Phase 1: 30-Day Free Trial] 
    - Goal: Rapid vendor acquisition.
    - Logic: Platform profit is set to 0%. 
    - Vendor Cost: Only the 2% Gateway Fee is deducted to cover direct transaction costs.
- [Phase 2: Post-Trial Standard]
    - Logic: Auto-transition to the full 5% commission once the `trialEndDate` (calculated at `startDate + 30 days`) is passed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. OPERATIONAL REVENUE: CANCELLATION PENALTIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To maintain a high quality of service and discourage vendors from cancelling confirmed orders (which frustrates students):

- [Penalty Rate]: 4% of the Order Value.
- [Applicability]: Applied only to orders where payment was "Confirmed" but the vendor manually "Cancelled" the order.
- [Rationale]:
    - 2% covers the non-refundable gateway fee incurred by the platform.
    - 2% acts as a "Service Inconvenience Fee" contributing to platform revenue.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE SETTLEMENT LIFECYCLE (FINANCE FLOW)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The backend calculates the "Net Payable" to vendors using the following logic:

[ Net Payable ] = [ Total Monthly Revenue ] - [ Total Deductions ]

Where [ Total Deductions ] = [ Gateway Fees ] + [ Platform Profits ] + [ Cancellation Penalties ]

- [Reconciliation]: The Super Admin panel provides a monthly "Finance Summary" showing the exact breakdown for each store.
- [Payouts]: Once the Super Admin clicks "Settle," a historical record is created in the `Settlements` collection, marking the amount as "Paid" to the vendor's UPI ID.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SCALABILITY & UNIT ECONOMICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- [Average Order Value (AOV)]: Specifically optimized for campus pricing (₹40 - ₹200).
- [Packaging Charges]: Vendors can configure a flat "Packaging Charge" per order. This charge is included in the total revenue calculation, helping vendors offset the cost of disposables while the platform maintains its margin.
- [Scalability]: As more markets (LIT, BH1, BH6) are added, the "Total Profit" scales linearly with the transaction volume without increasing infrastructure overhead significantly.
